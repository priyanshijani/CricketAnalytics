## Defects found and fixed during testing

| Defect | Fix |
|---|---|
| On AMP, partnerships came out scrambled (4,913 rows instead of 1,839) | AMP does not keep record order across tools, and a Multi-Row Formula grouped by over emits groups out of global order. A Sort now sits immediately before each Multi-Row chain; results verified identical to the E1 engine. |
| Load-row routing error check was wired to the wrong Filter anchor | Connected to the last filter's False output. |
| A batsman dismissed twice in the same innings (source data, 15 cases, not planted) | Found by the warehouse reconciliation; added DQ rule `batsman_dismissed_twice` (Flagged). |
| A rejected innings (M0006) left deliveries with no parent innings | Rejected innings rules now hold the match's ball-by-ball (`HoldsBallByBall = 1`). |
